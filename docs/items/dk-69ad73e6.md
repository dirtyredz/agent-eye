# Camera preview is very small on the phone (v1.3.1)

Owner report 2026-10-27: after the v1.3.1 UI polish the preview is 'super small' on the phone. Emulator screenshots (320x640) showed it slightly larger than 1.3.0, so not reproduced there.

Suspects (ui/capture/CameraLayout.kt, CameraScreen.kt): portrait reserves a fixed 216dp bottom panel + 64dp top bar + margins and FIT-scales the frame into what remains, instead of letting panels overlay the letterbox bars; landscape reserves a 280-320dp side panel; a wide sensor frame shown upright under whole-frame FIT (plan section 8) compounds it.

Next: owner sends portrait and landscape screenshots from 1.3.2 (screenshots allowed); fix from those. Keep whole-frame FIT and full-frame snapshots (owner decision); likely direction is panels overlaying the bars so the preview uses the full width.
